01 / profile

Curious about the layer between an idea and a working system.

I am Vedant, a computer scientist who enjoys moving between abstraction levels: from compiler IRs and network silicon to learning systems and the physical world. My work tends to be practical, research-minded, and shaped by a simple question: what would make this robust enough to use?

I graduated from the University of Alberta with a B.Sc. Honors in Computer Science, and I am happiest in teams that value clear thinking, careful experiments, and software that earns its complexity.
